# WikiBot

Pull a random, interesting Wikipedia article like a card from a library card catalog:
a picture, the title, and the opening of the article.

WikiBot is a beloved old project, first written on May 31, 2015 as a little Python
command-line bot that asked "Would you like to view...?" and opened random articles in
your browser. It has since had some love: it's now a single web page with pictures,
summaries, themes and a saved list, and it still has the same idea at heart.

## How to use it

**Double-click `WikiBot.html`.** It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or
Safari). Nothing to install. It needs an internet connection, because the articles come
live from Wikipedia.

To share it, send someone the `WikiBot.html` file (by email, a USB stick, or a shared
folder) and they double-click it too.

| Button | Key | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Draw another card | → or N | A new random article |
| Back | ← or B | The card you saw before |
| Save | S | Keeps it in your **Saved** list (press again to remove) |
| Read it | O | Opens the full article on Wikipedia |
| Theme | T | Picks a look (T flips through them) |

- **Only articles with pictures**: turn this off to include text-only articles too.
- **Language menu**: draw cards from 10 different Wikipedias.
- **Themes**: Auto (follows your computer's light or dark mode), Catalog, Midnight,
  Library, Botanical, Blueprint and Terminal. Terminal is a nod to the original
  command-line WikiBot. Your choice is remembered.

WikiBot skips the dull stuff: disambiguation pages, "List of..." pages and tiny stub
articles. It also loads the next card in the background while you read, so it shows up
right away.

Saved cards and your theme stay in that browser on that computer.
